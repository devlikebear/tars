TARS Remote is an iPhone app that connects to TARS running on your own computers. This policy says what the app does with your information.

## We collect nothing

The developer of TARS Remote does not collect, receive or store any information from the app. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting service and no tracking. It does not contact any server operated by the developer.

## What stays on your phone

- **Paired desktops.** For each desktop you pair, the app keeps its address, its name and a device token that desktop issued. They are stored in the iOS Keychain on this device only. They are not synced to iCloud.
- **Ideas.** Ideas you write are stored in the iOS Keychain on this device until you delete them.

Removing a desktop in the app deletes its entry from the phone. Like other Keychain items, these can remain after the app is deleted; remove the desktops and ideas in the app first, or revoke the phone on the desktop, to end access.

## What is sent, and to whom

The app sends requests only to the desktops you paired, over the network connection you set up (for example Tailscale). Those requests carry what you do in the app: your approvals and decisions, instructions you type, and ideas you send as tasks. They go directly to your own computer. What your computer then does with them, including which AI model provider it uses, is governed by your TARS configuration on that computer and that provider's terms, not by this app.

The built-in demo desk runs entirely inside the app and sends nothing.

## Children

The app is a developer tool and is not directed at children. It collects no information from anyone.

## Changes

If a later version changes what this policy describes, the policy will be updated and its effective date changed.
